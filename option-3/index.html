About Us

LeTech International Ltd

Specialist developer and supplier of unique premium materials for leather care and restoration. Our head office and production facilities are in Northampton, the leather capital of the world.

We work alongside the leading leather institutions in the UK, the BLC Leather Technology Centre and the University of Northampton, both based in Northampton and both our partners in leather technology. That is what allows us to create some of the most innovative leather care products in the world.

LeTech India is growing fast, bringing professional cleaning, protection and restoration to more cities every quarter.
